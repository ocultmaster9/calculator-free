The complete betting calculator toolkit

Whether you are working out an accumulator, splitting a each-way horse bet, or checking whether a Lucky 15 is worth the extra outlay, these calculators do the math for you. Betting math is simple in principle — multiply your odds, multiply by your stake — but system bets, place terms and commission make it fiddly to do in your head. Each tool below handles one job well.

Multiples & accumulators

A double combines two selections, a treble three, and an accumulator four or more. Every leg must win; the odds multiply together, which is why a small stake can return a large amount.

Full-cover system bets

Full-cover bets combine your selections into every possible multiple so you still get a return when only some of them win. In order of size: Trixie (3 selections, 4 bets), Patent (3, 7 with singles), Yankee (4, 11), Lucky 15 (4, 15), Canadian (5, 26), Lucky 31 (5, 31), Heinz (6, 57), Lucky 63 (6, 63) and the giant Goliath (8, 247). The "Lucky" bets and Patent include singles, so a single winner still returns.

Value & staking tools

The Kelly criterion tells you what fraction of your bankroll to stake based on your edge. Matched betting and arbitrage calculators show how to back and lay (or bet both sides across bookmakers) to lock in a guaranteed result. The odds converter switches between decimal, fractional and American formats and shows implied probability.

Casino odds

For card games, the poker pot odds calculator tells you the equity you need to make a profitable call, the blackjack basic strategy calculator gives the optimal play for every hand, and the card counting tool converts a Hi-Lo running count into a true count.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a bet calculator?+
A bet calculator works out the returns, profit and total odds on a bet before you place it. Enter your stake and odds and it shows exactly what a winning bet would pay — for singles, accumulators, system bets like Lucky 15, and more.
Which betting calculator should I use?+
Use the accumulator or double/treble tools for standard multiples, each-way for horse racing, full-cover system bets (Trixie, Patent, Yankee, Lucky 15/31/63, Canadian, Heinz, Goliath) when you want cover for partial winners, the Kelly criterion for staking, and matched betting or arbitrage to lock in value.
Are these calculators free?+
Yes. Every calculator here is completely free, works in your browser with no sign-up, and is purely informational to help you understand potential returns.
Do you take bets?+
No. calculator-free.com is not a bookmaker and does not accept bets or handle money. These are math tools that show what a bet would return.
