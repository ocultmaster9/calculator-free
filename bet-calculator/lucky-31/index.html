How the Lucky 31 Calculator Works

A Lucky 31 is a full-cover bet on 5 selections made up of 31 bets: 5 singles, 10 doubles, 10 trebles, 5 four-folds and 1 five-fold. Including the singles means a single winner still returns, and most bookmakers add bonuses.

The 31 bets

  • 5 singles
  • 10 doubles
  • 10 trebles
  • 5 four-folds
  • 1 five-fold

Worked example

A $1 Lucky 31 (total stake $31) on five selections at 2.00, 3.00, 4.00, 5.00 and 6.00 that all win returns about $2,519 before bonuses. Bonuses: single winner often paid at double odds, plus a 10% all-winners bonus.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Lucky 31?+
A Lucky 31 is 31 bets on 5 selections: 5 singles, 10 doubles, 10 trebles, 5 four-folds and a five-fold. A single winner still returns.
How much does a Lucky 31 cost?+
31 times your unit stake. A $1 Lucky 31 costs $31.
What bonuses apply?+
Commonly double the odds if only one selection wins, and a 10% bonus if all five win. Terms vary by bookmaker.
Lucky 31 vs Canadian?+
A Canadian (Super Yankee) is the same 5 selections without the singles — 26 bets — so the Lucky 31 adds single-winner cover.
