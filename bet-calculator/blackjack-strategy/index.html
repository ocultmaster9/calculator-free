How the Blackjack Basic Strategy Calculator Works

Basic strategy is the mathematically optimal way to play every blackjack hand. For any combination of your hand and the dealer’s up card, one decision — hit, stand, double, split or surrender — gives the best long-run result. Pick your hand and the dealer’s card above and the calculator shows that move instantly.

Playing perfect basic strategy reduces the house edge to roughly 0.5% under common rules — far better than the 2–4% a casual player typically gives up. This tool uses the standard chart for 4–8 decks where the dealer stands on soft 17, doubling after a split is allowed, and late surrender is available.

The three kinds of hands

  • Hard totals — no ace, or an ace forced to count as 1. The tough decisions are the “stiff” hands (12–16) against the dealer’s card.
  • Soft hands — a hand with an ace counted as 11 (e.g. A-7 = soft 18). Because the ace can drop to 1, you can hit or double these more aggressively.
  • Pairs — two cards of the same rank. Aces and eights always split; others depend on the dealer’s card.

Worked example

You hold a hard 16 and the dealer shows a 10 — the worst spot in blackjack. Basic strategy says surrender if you can (or hit if surrender isn’t offered), because you lose less over time than by standing. Against a dealer 6, the same 16 says stand — the dealer is likely to bust.

Can basic strategy beat the house?

No — and it’s important to be honest about that. Perfect basic strategy minimises the house edge to about 0.5%, but it doesn’t erase it. The only proven way to turn a real edge in your favour is card counting — tracking the ratio of high to low cards and raising your bet when the deck is rich. Our card counting calculator shows how a running count becomes a true count and a player edge. Basic strategy first, counting second — that’s the real “beat the house” toolkit.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is blackjack basic strategy?+
Basic strategy is the mathematically proven best decision for every possible hand against every dealer up card. Followed perfectly it cuts the house edge to about 0.5%.
Does this calculator guarantee I win?+
No. Basic strategy minimises the house edge but does not overcome it — blackjack still slightly favours the house. It is a training aid for optimal play, not a promise of profit.
What rules does the calculator assume?+
The standard 4–8 deck chart: dealer stands on soft 17, double after split allowed, and late surrender available — the most common casino rules.
What actually beats blackjack?+
Card counting. Basic strategy plays each hand optimally, but only counting cards and raising your bet when the deck is favourable gives a real long-term edge. See our card counting calculator.
What is a soft hand?+
A hand containing an ace counted as 11 — for example A-7 is a ‘soft 18’. Because the ace can also count as 1, soft hands can be hit or doubled more aggressively without busting.
