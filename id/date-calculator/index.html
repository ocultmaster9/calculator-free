How to Calculate Days Between Two Dates

The simplest way to find days between dates is to convert both dates to a day count from a fixed reference point, then subtract. Our calculator does this automatically for any pair of dates.

Common uses for date calculations: counting days until a deadline or event, calculating someone's age in days, determining contract durations, figuring out when a payment is due, and calculating how many shopping days until a holiday.

Adding months is trickier than adding days because months have different lengths. Adding 1 month to January 31 is ambiguous — some systems use February 28, others use the last day of the target month. Our calculator uses the standard approach of adjusting to the last valid day of the month.

Useful Date Facts

  • A regular year has 365 days; a leap year has 366 days
  • Leap years occur every 4 years, except centuries not divisible by 400 (2000 was a leap year; 1900 was not)
  • There are 52 weeks + 1 day in a regular year; 52 weeks + 2 days in a leap year
  • The average month length is 30.44 days (365.25 ÷ 12)

Pertanyaan Umum

How many days are there between two dates?+
Subtract the earlier date from the later date. Our calculator shows the result in days, weeks, months, and years simultaneously. Example: from January 1 to December 31 of the same year = 364 days.
How do I add 90 days to a date?+
Enter your start date and type 90 in the "Add/Subtract Days" tab. Our calculator accounts for different month lengths and leap years automatically.
How many working/business days between two dates?+
Business day calculation requires knowing local holidays. Our calculator shows calendar days. To convert: subtract weekends (2 days per week) and estimate roughly 10–12 public holidays per year for the US.
What is a Julian date?+
A Julian date is a continuous count of days since noon on January 1, 4713 BC, used in astronomy. It is not the same as the Julian calendar. For everyday date arithmetic, standard calendar math is what you need.

Menghitung Jumlah Hari Antara Dua Tanggal

Aritmetika tanggal menghitung jumlah hari yang memisahkan dua tanggal kalender, dengan memperhitungkan panjang bulan dan tahun kabisat (setiap 4 tahun, kecuali tahun abad yang tidak habis dibagi 400). Kalkulator umumnya mengubah setiap tanggal menjadi nomor hari berurutan lalu menguranginya.

Contoh perhitungan

Dari 1 Januari 2026 hingga 4 Juli 2026 adalah 184 hari: 30 sisa hari di Januari + 28 (Februari 2026 bukan tahun kabisat) + 31 + 30 + 31 + 30 + 4.

Pertanyaan yang sering diajukan

Penghitungan inklusif atau eksklusif? Konvensinya tidak menghitung tanggal awal dan menghitung tanggal akhir; tenggat dalam kontrak bisa mengaturnya berbeda, jadi baca ketentuannya.

Hari kerja? Hitungan hari kerja tidak memasukkan Sabtu dan Minggu serta umumnya hari libur nasional, yang berbeda-beda tiap negara.

Mengapa panjang bulan berbeda-beda? Warisan kalender Romawi — dan justru itulah sebabnya penghitungan hari otomatis mengalahkan hitungan di kepala untuk hal-hal penting.
